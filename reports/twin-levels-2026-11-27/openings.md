# Openings

Generated by `jiro-tools playtest --openings`: seed 1, 1000 run nonces per level, those of `jiro-tools playtest`. An opening is a straight line of cuts (a column from the bottom border, a row from a side border, or every door of one bar), walked to on the rail and cut with a rest on each claimed cell. Blind: from tick 0 with no waiting. Timed: each cut waits for the first tick from which it closes without a hit, which the core's determinism allows. Large capture: 3000 bp or more.

The best fixed line is the one plan that works on the most seeds; the other blind columns take, on each seed, the best of all plans.
