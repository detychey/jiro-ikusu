# Paid levels on rules version 5 (plan M5.16)

Measured on 2026-11-28 on the branch of plan M5.16, merged with `main` at `4206647`: rules version 5 (plan M5.20), with the twin-boss levels 10, 13, 15 and 16 of plan M5.19. Tools: the release `jiro-tools` (Rust 1.98.0), and the release `zk-host` of RISC Zero 3.1.0 with verify image id `0x9a81d92cbf95714f2637b0a873dd403d2c498ba3db29a4308900e37088fecbdd`, `RISC0_DEV_MODE` unset, executor only (no proof). The players are scripted bots standing in for people, not people.

The paid copies are the 16 levels with 3 more lives and a target of 5,000 bp (README §8.2, D17). `jiro-tools level build levels/src levels` writes them to `levels/paid/` along with the 16 levels, and [`levels/README.md`](../levels/README.md) lists their ids. On a twin-boss copy, the separation floor is half the copy's target, 2,500 bp (README §4.8).

## Summary

- **The bot edge ρ on the paid copies** is 1.020 for the reference mix, 1.063 with 20 % novices and 1.111 with 40 %. Rules version 2 gave 1.007, 1.105 and 1.223, and rules version 3 gave 1.015, 1.048 and 1.084 (this item's first measurement, with levels 10, 13, 15 and 16 still single-boss).
- **The twin-boss copies cause the change from rules version 3.** The 12 single-boss copies measure the same as on rules version 3, row for row. Copies 10, 13, 15 and 16 are now the four hardest for novices: a population of 40 % novices averages 0.72 to 0.83 on them.
- **People's average ticket on the paid copies** is 0.980, 0.939 or 0.899, above D17's target of 0.80 to 0.85. The scripted players clear 89 % to 96 % of their games and run out of lives in 1.4 % to 9.7 % of them. People will differ, and testnet-1's level review (README §8.4, D21) measures them.
- **On the 16 levels, ρ** is 1.525 for the reference mix, 1.727 with 20 % novices and 1.991 with 40 %. Rules version 2 gave 1.405 and 2.089; there is no published v2 figure for 20 % novices.
- **Ticks and cost of a run.** A run on the paid copies takes 1,566 to 2,034 ticks and, on average, 37 to 43 million proving cycles: about 0.021 to 0.024 USD of GPU time at M5.9's model. The heaviest of 400 runs took 114 million. Proving and settling an average run of the reference mix costs about 0.039 to 0.055 USD, against README §8.4's cap of 0.048 USD.

## 1. The bot edge

The figures come from two reports, both generated by `jiro-tools playtest --edge` with 1,000 Training runs of seed 1 per policy and level:

- [`edge-38a056ae1365.md`](edge-38a056ae1365.md): the paid copies;
- [`edge-eebbc0206140.md`](edge-eebbc0206140.md): the 16 levels.

The definitions:

- A game's ticket is min(claim, target) / target, and 0 when the lives ran out. A clear by separation at the floor raises the claim to the target, so it also earns a ticket of 1 (README §7.3).
- A run draws six different levels, so people's average ticket w̄ is the mean over the levels.
- A perfect bot's ticket is 1, so ρ = 1 / w̄.
- The novices are edge-nibbler and random-walk in equal shares, as in the reward research of 2026-11-25. The splitter policy of plan M5.19 is not one of the scripted people.

| People | Paid copies: ρ, v2 | ρ, v3 | v5: w̄ | **ρ** | d | 16 levels: ρ, v2 | ρ, v3 | v5: w̄ | **ρ** | d |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| the reference mix (bold, committer) | 1.007 | 1.015 | 0.980 | **1.020** | 1.4 % | 1.405 | 1.544 | 0.655 | **1.525** | 23.4 % |
| 20 % novices | 1.105 | 1.048 | 0.939 | **1.063** | 5.5 % | – | 1.751 | 0.578 | **1.727** | 33.3 % |
| 40 % novices | 1.223 | 1.084 | 0.899 | **1.111** | 9.7 % | 2.089 | 2.024 | 0.502 | **1.991** | 43.2 % |

The v2 columns are the research's figures, as README §8.4 quotes them; the research's method is not in the repository. The v3 and v5 columns are this tool's.

ρ ≈ 1 / (1 − d) holds within 0.6 % on the paid copies, because almost every game that does not run out of lives clears.

By policy, on the paid copies, the mean tickets are:

- bold 0.970;
- committer 0.989 (it commits rather than lose its last life);
- edge-nibbler 0.797;
- random-walk 0.759.

The hardest copies for a population of 40 % novices are 16 doom (0.718), 15 swarm (0.723), 12 ring (0.801), 13 trio (0.802) and 10 maze (0.834). The reference mix's lowest is 16 doom, 0.933.

## 3. Ticks and proving cycles per run

**Ticks** are the edge report's, over 16,000 games per policy.

**Proving cycles** are measured on 400 runs:

- **The runs:** runs 0 to 99 of seed 1, each played by each policy. Run k has the run nonce keccak256("jiro.playtest." ‖ 1 as 8 bytes ‖ k as 4 bytes), as the playtest numbers its runs.
- **How:** each run is played on the levels its draw names, under Challenge headers, then executed as one `verifyRun` by `zk-host execute-run`. Each run's ticks and cycles are in [`paid-levels-2026-11-28/runs.txt`](paid-levels-2026-11-28/runs.txt).
- **Counting:** proving cycles are the segments' sizes padded to 2^20.
- **Cost:** GPU time and cost follow M5.9's model, 7.3 s + 2.15 s a segment on a rented RTX 4090 at 0.89 USD an hour, without a session's set-up.

The populations' rows weigh the policies' means as the edge report does. Their ticks are the edge report's; the 400 runs come within 2 % of them (1,541, 1,792 and 2,043).

On rules version 3, before the twin bosses, the same 400 runs averaged 39.3, 42.1 and 45.0 million proving cycles. Under rules version 2, M5.11's model put the reference mix's mean run at 47 million ([`cycle-cap-2026-11-27.md`](cycle-cap-2026-11-27.md) §7). Every run measured here stays far below the work budget's cap.

**The house condition** (README §8.4, D20), at README §8.4's references:

- Settling a run costs 530,256 to about 1,000,000 gas, or 0.018 to 0.034 USD.
- Proving and settling an average run therefore costs 0.039 to 0.055 USD for the reference mix, and 0.042 to 0.058 USD with 40 % novices.
- The cap is 0.048 USD on a 0.30 USD run, so it holds the average run only at the low end of the settlement gas.
- On testnet-1 the operator settles every scoring run, whatever it costs, and records the real costs.

## Reproduce

```sh
cargo run -p jiro-tools --release --locked -- level build levels/src levels
cargo run -p jiro-tools --release --locked -- level validate levels/paid/*.bin
cargo run -p jiro-tools --release --locked -- playtest --levels levels/paid --edge reports/edge-38a056ae1365.md
cargo run -p jiro-tools --release --locked -- playtest --levels levels --edge reports/edge-eebbc0206140.md
# For each policy and k from 0 to 99, with N the run nonce of run k above (zk-host from referee/zk):
for i in 0 1 2 3 4 5; do
  target/release/jiro-tools playtest --levels levels/paid --policy <policy> --nonce N --level-index $i --out run/$i.json
done
referee/zk/target/release/zk-host execute-run run/0.json run/1.json run/2.json run/3.json run/4.json run/5.json
```

Each `.json` output is a game vector of the drawn level, with its native Outcome, which `zk-host` checks.
