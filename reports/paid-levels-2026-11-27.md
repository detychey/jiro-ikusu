# Paid levels on rules version 3 (plan M5.16)

Measured on 2026-11-27 on the branch of plan M5.16, stacked on M5.12's `5a0672f` (rules version 3), with the release `jiro-tools` (Rust 1.98.0) and the release `zk-host` of RISC Zero 3.1.0, verify image id `0x7e1988ace7094a3f7a5b7bf04a8196bc15e4763b3122e7fd5d6a9fdad44588f0`, `RISC0_DEV_MODE` unset, executor only (no proof). The players are scripted bots standing in for people, not people.

The paid copies are the 16 levels with 3 more lives and a target of 5,000 bp (README §8.2, D17), which `jiro-tools level build levels/src levels` writes to `levels/paid/` with the 16 levels; [`levels/README.md`](../levels/README.md) lists their ids. Levels 10, 13, 15 and 16 become twin-boss levels in M5.19: their paid copies, their rows below and `seasons/testnet-1.json`'s ids are measured and written again then. The 12 single-boss levels play the same on rules version 4.

## Summary

- **The bot edge ρ on the paid copies is 1.015 for the reference mix, 1.048 with 20 % novices and 1.084 with 40 %**, against 1.007, 1.105 and 1.223 on rules version 2. Rules version 3 lowered it for the populations with novices and raised it slightly for the reference mix. This report does not separate the causes, the shield's removal, the fast cut always on and the work budget, and the research's own method is not in the repository: the v3 figures are the tool's, which the report states.
- **People's average ticket on the paid copies is 0.984, 0.953 or 0.921**, above D17's target of 0.80 to 0.85. The scripted players clear 91 % to 97 % of their games and run out of lives in 1 % to 7.5 % of them. People will differ: testnet-1's level review (README §8.4, D21) measures them.
- **On the 16 levels, ρ is 1.544 for the reference mix, 1.751 with 20 % novices and 2.024 with 40 %**, against 1.405 and 2.089 on rules version 2.
- **A run on the paid copies takes 1,500 to 1,980 ticks and 39 to 45 million proving cycles on average**, about 0.022 to 0.025 USD of GPU time at M5.9's model, and at most 130 million in 400 runs. Against README §8.4's cap of 0.048 USD, proving and settling an average run of the reference mix costs about 0.040 to 0.056 USD.

## 1. The bot edge

From [`edge-e23a5ed93c3f.md`](edge-e23a5ed93c3f.md), the paid copies, and [`edge-b0b72cb41408.md`](edge-b0b72cb41408.md), the 16 levels, both generated by `jiro-tools playtest --edge`: 1,000 Training runs of seed 1 per policy and level. A game's ticket is min(claim, target) / target, and 0 when the lives ran out. A run draws six different levels, so people's average ticket w̄ is the mean over the levels, and a perfect bot's ticket is 1, so ρ = 1 / w̄. The novices are edge-nibbler and random-walk in equal shares, as in the reward research of 2026-11-25.

| People | Paid copies, v2 | Paid copies, v3: w̄ | ρ | d | 16 levels, v2 | 16 levels, v3: w̄ | ρ | d |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| the reference mix (bold, committer) | 1.007 | 0.984 | **1.015** | 1.0 % | 1.405 | 0.647 | **1.544** | 23.3 % |
| 20 % novices | 1.105 | 0.953 | **1.048** | 4.3 % | – | 0.570 | **1.751** | 33.4 % |
| 40 % novices | 1.223 | 0.921 | **1.084** | 7.5 % | 2.089 | 0.494 | **2.024** | 43.4 % |

The v2 columns are the research's figures quoted by README §8.4, with no figure published for the 16 levels at 20 % novices. ρ ≈ 1 / (1 − d) holds within 0.5 % on the paid copies: almost every game that does not run out of lives clears.

By policy, on the paid copies: bold's mean ticket is 0.978 and committer's 0.991, which commits rather than lose its last life; edge-nibbler's 0.847 and random-walk's 0.807. The hardest copies for the novices are 12 ring, 15 swarm and 16 doom, where a population of 40 % novices averages 0.80, 0.81 and 0.79; the reference mix's lowest is 16 doom, 0.949.

## 3. Ticks and proving cycles per run

Ticks are the edge report's, over 16,000 games per policy. Proving cycles are measured: runs 0 to 99 of seed 1 (run k has the run nonce keccak256("jiro.playtest." ‖ 1 as 8 bytes ‖ k as 4 bytes), the playtest's), each played by each policy on the levels its draw names, under Challenge headers, then executed as one `verifyRun` by `zk-host execute-run`: 400 runs, each with its ticks and cycles in [`paid-levels-2026-11-27/runs.txt`](paid-levels-2026-11-27/runs.txt). Proving cycles are the segments' padded sizes at 2^20; GPU time and cost follow M5.9's model, 7.3 s + 2.15 s a segment on a rented RTX 4090 at 0.89 USD an hour, without a session's set-up.

The populations' rows weigh the policies' means as the edge report does; their ticks are the edge report's, which the 400 runs match within 1 % (1,480, 1,739 and 1,999). Under rules version 2, M5.11's model put the reference mix's mean run on the paid copies at 47 million proving cycles ([`cycle-cap-2026-11-27.md`](cycle-cap-2026-11-27.md) §7); every run measured here stays far below the work budget's cap of 370 million.

**The house condition** (README §8.4, D20) at README §8.4's references: settling a run costs 530,256 to about 1,000,000 gas, 0.018 to 0.034 USD, so proving and settling an average run costs 0.040 to 0.056 USD for the reference mix and 0.043 to 0.059 USD with 40 % novices, against a cap of 0.048 USD on a 0.30 USD run: the cap holds the average run at the low end of the settlement gas only. On testnet-1 the operator settles every scoring run, whatever it costs, and records the real costs.

## Reproduce

```sh
cargo run -p jiro-tools --release --locked -- level build levels/src levels
cargo run -p jiro-tools --release --locked -- level validate levels/paid/*.bin
cargo run -p jiro-tools --release --locked -- playtest --levels levels/paid --edge reports/edge-e23a5ed93c3f.md
cargo run -p jiro-tools --release --locked -- playtest --levels levels --edge reports/edge-b0b72cb41408.md
# For each policy and k from 0 to 99, with N the run nonce of run k above (zk-host from referee/zk):
for i in 0 1 2 3 4 5; do
  target/release/jiro-tools playtest --levels levels/paid --policy <policy> --nonce N --level-index $i --out run/$i.json
done
referee/zk/target/release/zk-host execute-run run/0.json run/1.json run/2.json run/3.json run/4.json run/5.json
```

Each `.json` out is a game vector of the drawn level, with its native Outcome, which `zk-host` checks.
