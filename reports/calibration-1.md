# calibration-1

Generated by `jiro-tools calibrate`: rules version 2, seed 1, 1000 runs per policy and level, on the run nonces of `jiro-tools playtest`. The reference mix (bold, committer) sets each level's weight from its median effective claim m (README §8.4): weight_bp = P × 10,000 / max(m, floor), rounded down, with P = 12000 points, the largest multiple of 1,000 that keeps every weight within a u16, and a floor of 500 bp. A median run therefore scores P points, or P − 1 by rounding, on every level. Effective claim: `claim_bp`, or 0 when the lives ran out (D5). Medians at index (n − 1) / 2 of the sorted runs.
