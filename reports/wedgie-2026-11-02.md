# wedgie Training app: timing and RAM (plan M3.9)

The Training app of `wedgie/app/`, with `jiro_core.mpy` of the same commit (18,180 bytes, sha256 `092c5a7f91494e2b3f0287cd43da2bc367de848a4ae25e4c8458607674b9468b`). Tools: MicroPython v1.29.0 (commit 0fd6c573) and its qemu port for the MICROBIT board (Cortex-M0, 236 KB heap), qemu-system-arm 8.2.2, arm-none-eabi-gcc 13.2.1, rustc 1.98.0. The wedgie's own numbers are round E1's, below.

## qemu

`python3 -I wedgie/scripts/qemu_app_check.py <firmware.elf> wedgie/app 137760`: the app's `run()`, with the heap filled down to the 137,760 bytes a wedgie reports free with no app, plays level 1 with scripted inputs (88 ticks, two captures, then Commit). PASS: the journal is the scripted keys' mask changes, and native replay gives the app's end (Committed), claim (677 bp), ticks (88) and score (1,049).

**RAM.**

| Free at `run()` | Result | Free after start | Largest free block after start |
|---|---|---|---|
| 137,760 | PASS | 20,192 | 3,279 |
| 129,250 | PASS | 11,808 | 1,351 |
| 128,500 | MemoryError allocating the 64 KB stack | | |
| 125,000 | MemoryError allocating the 64 KB stack | | |

The margin is about 8.5 KB. Below 129,000, compiling `jiro.py` at import leaves its code in the heap's one large block, and no 64 KB piece remains: an exception, not a freeze. M3.10 is not needed.

**Time.** The app measures with `time.ticks_us()`. Under `-icount shift=3` one microsecond of the qemu port is 195.3 instructions (`wedgie/README.md`, qemu's clock); a wedgie runs 125 instructions per microsecond at one instruction per cycle, more cycles per instruction on loads, taken branches and flash cache misses.

| Level | Worst step | Worst draw | Worst tick | Overruns | Most rows pushed in a tick |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Open (the play test) | 6,505 µs: 1.27 M instructions, 10.2 ms at 125 MHz | 1,357 µs: 0.27 M, 2.1 ms | 7,136 µs: 1.39 M, 11.2 ms | 0 | 104 |
| 10 Maze (same inputs, a 32 % capture) | 6,082 µs: 1.19 M, 9.5 ms | 2,150 µs: 0.42 M, 3.4 ms | 6,941 µs: 1.36 M, 10.8 ms | 0 | 124 |
| 15 Swarm (three minions) | 6,533 µs: 1.28 M, 10.2 ms | 2,114 µs: 0.41 M, 3.3 ms | 7,404 µs: 1.45 M, 11.6 ms | 0 | 132 |
| 16 Doom | 6,540 µs: 1.28 M, 10.2 ms | 1,623 µs: 0.32 M, 2.5 ms | 7,427 µs: 1.45 M, 11.6 ms | 0 | 136 |

The worst step is a capture tick: the boss-zone fill runs over the open cells, whatever the capture's size. qemu's `show_rect` pushes nothing, so a wedgie adds the push: about 45 µs a row (wedgie.dev/code.md), 4.7 to 6.1 ms for the rows above. Expected on a wedgie: a worst tick of about 15 to 30 ms against the 66.7 ms tick and the 50 ms of round E1, and no overrun in play. All 16 levels started, passed the level-id check, played and scored on qemu with the same inputs (an ad-hoc run of the play test's script with another level picked), every one within 1.45 M instructions a tick, 136 rows pushed and no overrun.
