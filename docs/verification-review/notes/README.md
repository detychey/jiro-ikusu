## What to know before reading

- **Paths.** `scratchpad/...` names the review's working directory, which was not kept. Only the files in this folder were. `<svm>` is the local solc install of svm.
- **Plan ids.** The escrow notes, `report/agents.md`, `report/cloud.md` and the first two checks in `report/review/` use draft ids from M5.8 to M5.14, written when M5.8 was the next free id. The plan has since used M5.8 for season operations and M5.9 for GPU proving. A draft id in these notes is not a plan item.
- **Words of the review.** The lead is the reviewer who ran the review and wrote the report. The brief is the task text the lead gave each researcher, designer or reviewer; its figures are the lead's. The addendum is the lead's list of eight corrected premises, given to the reviewers of the optimistic designs. Its items 1 to 7 are facts and constraints that they were not asked to recheck. Its item 8 is the owner's direction: a validity proof for every paid-out run, made on a GPU by an open, paid service. The owner's questions are in section 7 of the report. `doc-rev40.md` and `doc-rev101.md` are drafts of the report.
- **Structured results.** Some notes point to a "structured result": the summary their author returned to the review's lead. For the reviews of the report, the full findings are in [`report/review/first-review-kept-findings.md`](report/review/first-review-kept-findings.md), [`report/review/second-check-findings.md`](report/review/second-check-findings.md), [`report/review/third-check-findings.md`](report/review/third-check-findings.md) and [`report/review/fourth-check-findings.md`](report/review/fourth-check-findings.md).
- **Later corrections.** [`report/cloud-check.md`](report/cloud-check.md) corrects [`report/cloud.md`](report/cloud.md), and [`report/recompute.py`](report/recompute.py) applies those corrections. [`report/agents-check.md`](report/agents-check.md) does the same for [`report/agents.md`](report/agents.md).
- **The prototype** in [`escrow/prototype/`](escrow/prototype/) holds only the new and changed Solidity files: `ProofBounty.sol`, the changed `ZkReferee.sol`, and their tests. They passed against copies of the repository's contracts at `f52a60a`. The repository does not build them. The eleven `ZkReferee` tests that read the old-format proof fixtures fail until the fixtures are proven again, and the prototype lacks the cap per run and the refund on a stopped verifier.
- **The faster flood fill** is [`audit/flood-fill-index-arithmetic.diff`](audit/flood-fill-index-arithmetic.diff), a scratch experiment against `core/src/grid.rs`. It reproduced every vector's outcome and trace hash, but it is not written to the core's rules: it uses `usize` and a cast.
- **The scratch tools** behind the audits' measurements are in [`audit/scratch-tools.tar.gz`](audit/scratch-tools.tar.gz), an archive so that their Rust sources and manifests stay out of the repository's checks. It holds:
  - the instruction counter: a bare-metal RV32IM build of the replay (`rv32/guest`, `rv32/guest-opt1`) and its interpreter (`rv32/emu`);
  - the cost-maximising policy (`nibbler`) and its 16 levels and journals (`data/nibble-bin`), the inputs for measuring that run on the real executor;
  - the checks that the faster flood fill reproduces every vector (`check-base`, `check-opt1`), and the event counts behind the cost model (`native-stats`);
  - the probe that restores a game from a state-hash preimage, for the option of a dispute narrowed to one tick (`restore-check`);
  - the timing and profiling scripts and the measured tables (`data/*.tsv`, `data/*.json`).

  Two scripts and five Rust manifests name the review's clone at `/home/user/jiro-ikusu`. Point them at your own clone before running them. The manifests of `check-opt1` and `rv32/guest-opt1` point instead at `scratch-tools/core-opt1/`, which the archive does not hold: copy your clone's `core/` there and apply [`audit/flood-fill-index-arithmetic.diff`](audit/flood-fill-index-arithmetic.diff) inside it with `patch -p2`.
