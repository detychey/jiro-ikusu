# Mechanics: level structure and progression

The 54 mechanic families of the axis *level structure and progression*: stages and progression: stage goals, boss stages, difficulty ramps, level select, world maps, unlocks, continues, story structure. The [taxonomy](../TAXONOMY.md) summarizes the axis and lists the 12 axes. **Games** counts the catalog games that show a family, as the merging agent counted them.

## Per-level enemy ramp

Each new level, screen or wave makes the same enemies harder: one more enemy (often exactly one more ball, Qix or atom per level), faster enemies, smarter homing, or a tighter resource such as a shorter timer or gap. The ramp follows a formula, usually linear, and adds no new rules. In io-territory games it appears as smarter, greedier or longer-ranging rival bots.

Games: 85 · lineages: qix, xonix, jezzball, gals-panic, io-territory, amidar-painter, other

Examples:

- **quix (QIX on Unix)** (c. 1982-1984): Each new screen adds one more Qix.
- **Amidar** (1982): More and faster enemies and more complex grids, and the Tracer starts following sooner.
- **Xonix** (1984): One more ball per level, and the balls get faster.
- **Frenzy** (1984): Chasers arrive on higher levels; levels 12 and 14 have fewer but much faster Leptons and no Chasers.
- **Gridd Worz** (1984): One more Drone per level, up to four, and one more random lethal obstacle.
- **JezzBall** (1992): Level 1 has 2 atoms and each level adds one.
- **Xonix (X11/Macintosh reimplementation)** (1995): One more flyer each level and one more eater every fifth level.
- **Lovely Girls** (1998): Later stages bring more aggressive enemies and a shorter time limit.
- **ONLINE QIX** (2007): The single-player roster grows from 3 enemies to 10.
- **Pretty Girls Panic!** (2016): Enemies grow stronger stage by stage.
- **Paper Land: Territory Grab** (2026): The ladder brings bigger maps and smarter, greedier rivals.
- **Neon Capture** (2026): An endless ladder of levels that get harder as you climb.

Variants:

- One more enemy per level, in a straight line: Xonix (1984) adds a ball and speeds them up; JezzBall starts with 2 atoms and adds one; KBounce has n+1 balls on level n; IceBreaker adds a penguin per iceberg.
- One more Qix per screen (quix); QIX (Japanese mobile) also makes them faster.
- Two enemy pools ramp at different rates: one more flyer every level and one more eater every fifth level (Xonix X11/Macintosh); n+1 sea balls and n land balls on level n (Xonix KolibriOS).
- Speed-only ramp: the line speeds up each level (Qix, Tilt type-in); a faster, more aggressive Statix on each screen (Statix).
- Smarter enemies: the Qix gets smarter and the Sparx count rises every few stages (Qix FM-7); the Qix always homes while you draw from screen 4 (Qix ZX Spectrum PCW type-in); the Bug homes more often and Blips come more often (Doodlebug); the Tracer starts following sooner (Amidar).
- Setpiece levels break the ramp with fewer but much faster enemies and no Chasers (Frenzy, levels 12 and 14).
- More balls, but smaller ones (Land Breaker).
- The ramp tightens the player's tools: shorter gap duration plus more boxes (Gapper); a shorter time limit (Lovely Girls).
- Obstacles ramp with the enemies: one more Drone per level, up to four, plus one more random lethal obstacle (Gridd Worz).
- The ramp runs in only one mode: Arcade keeps adding enemies (The Filling, Blobbed Away); Infinity mode (Glowfield); endless arenas (NoSpace Arena).
- The single-player roster grows from 3 enemies to 10 (ONLINE QIX).
- Endless ladder with no cap (Neon Capture, RetroQix, Tarantula vs Snake, Containment by hau-game-studio).
- Rival bots get smarter and greedier, or range further, each round (Paper Land: Territory Grab, 땅따먹기왕); rising enemy pressure (SwarmBlitz).
- The ramp runs across a host game's classes (Art Class, Bully minigame).
- Counter-example: no extra ball per level (Jezzball 82, TI-BASIC).

## Worlds, chapters and themed stage groups

- **PacXon New Realms** (2024): 192 levels across 4 locations.

## Area-percentage clear threshold

A stage, match or level clears as soon as the claimed share of the field reaches a set percentage. 75-80% is the classic standard. The threshold may be fixed, vary by stage, arena or difficulty, or exclude obstacles. In multiplayer arenas it acts as an instant win.

Games: 27 · lineages: qix, volfied, xonix, io-territory

Examples:

- **Kix (BBC Micro)** (1986): 20 quadrants, each needing 75% coverage.
- **Volfied** (1989): 80% over 16 stages.
- **Volfied (PC Engine port)** (1989): 75% over 40 rounds.
- **Tarantula vs Snake** (2011): Spin a 'sufficiently large' web.
- **Mokoko X** (2022): The required percentage is set by difficulty.
- **Hexanaut.io** (2022): 20% makes you king; then hold it for 3 minutes.
- **The Zamio** (2023): At least 80% captures the whole building.
- **Pulsebound: The Expanse** (unknown): The mission ends at 30% of the map secured.
- **Territory Raider** (2026): 80%, with rocks excluded.
- **Volfi: Cyber Territory Arcade** (2026): 75% to 85% by arena.
- **Turf War** (2026): Instant win at 35%.
- **Paper Land: Territory Grab** (2026): Timed targets of 10%, 15% and 20%.

Variants:

- Fixed 80% over 16 stages (Volfied); 75% over 40 rounds on the PC Engine port; 80% is common across descendants (Volfield, ArcField, CUT Arcade, Gravebloom); 75% in Area 99.9%.
- Threshold varies by arena, from 75% to 85% (Volfi: Cyber Territory Arcade).
- The chosen difficulty sets the threshold (Mokoko X).
- Uncapturable rocks are left out of the count (Territory Raider).
- Reaching 80% captures the whole remaining building (The Zamio).
- A low mission threshold: the mission ends at 30% secured (Pulsebound: The Expanse).
- The percentage is one of three graded objectives (Pakal).
- Each of 20 quadrants needs 75% (Kix, BBC Micro); 3 lives to reach 75% (Xonix Girls).
- An unspecified 'sufficiently large' claim (Tarantula vs Snake).
- Instant multiplayer win at a share: 35% (Turf War), 50% in 1v1 (Warix), 60% (Loop: Land Grab); io targets range from 20% to 85% (Pengu Floe 40%, Snake Conquest 85%).
- Threshold, then hold: 20% makes you king, and you must then hold it for 3 minutes (Hexanaut.io).
- Timed escalating targets of 10%, 15% and 20% (Paper Land: Territory Grab); a domination goal before the timer ends (BeastGrid); boards cleared above a target share (땅따먹기왕).
