# Mechanics: items and power-ups

The 54 mechanic families of the axis *items and power-ups*: pickups and power-ups: how they appear (captured boxes, random drops) and what they do (speed, laser, shield, time stop, extra life, enemy slow, area bombs). The [taxonomy](../TAXONOMY.md) summarizes the axis and lists the 12 axes. **Games** counts the catalog games that show a family, as the merging agent counted them.

## Player speed boost

A pickup, crate, totem, skill or upgrade raises the player's movement or drawing speed. The boost usually lasts a set time, sometimes until a miss or stage clear, and sometimes for good. It shortens the time a trail is exposed. Some games pair it with a speed-down penalty.

Games: 56 · lineages: qix, xonix, volfied, gals-panic, io-territory

Examples:

- **Volfied** (1989): S speed lasts until stage clear or a miss.
- **Dancing Eyes** (1996): S speed item.
- **Coach Tzabar** (1997): A temporary speed item from boxes burst by capture.

- **AirXonix** (2000): Speed-up and slow-down modifiers.
- **Neo Xonix** (2010): Bonus 'S' doubles speed.
