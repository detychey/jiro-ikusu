# Mechanics: modes and meta

The 44 mechanic families of the axis *modes and meta*: game modes (puzzle, time attack, endless, daily, editor, user levels), meta-progression, collections, economy, gacha. The [taxonomy](../TAXONOMY.md) summarizes the axis and lists the 12 axes. **Games** counts the catalog games that show a family, as the merging agent counted them.

## Endless mode

Play has no final level. Stages, waves, images or a single board keep coming with rising difficulty (more enemies, more speed, a tighter board) until the player fails, and the score is how far or how long the player lasted. Some games add it after the campaign or unlock it, and in a few you cannot die.

Games: 38 · lineages: qix, xonix, volfied, jezzball, io-territory, gals-panic, other

Examples:

- **Bix** (2008): The Rift: a hidden endless climb after level 100.
- **Critter Round-Up** (2008): Endless Marathon mode for up to 4 players.
- **Yoshi Touch & Go** (2005): Marathon mode beside score and time attack.
- **Spider: The Secret of Bryce Manor** (2009): Hunger, an endless mode.
- **Delta Light 2** (2025): Infinite Mode after the campaign.
- **Territory: Claim & Conquer** (2026): Endless is one board that keeps tightening.
- **Xonix-Game-Development-Project / xonic-game-cpp-sfml** (2025-2026): Continuous mode: one more enemy every 30 seconds and a speed increase every 20 seconds.
- **Gotta Xonix** (2026): Endless mode in waves, with an upgrade pick every five waves.
- **Photo Slice** (2026): Clearing all hazards opens the whole photo, then the run continues on a new photo.
- **FillBottom** (2020): Keep filling the floor as blocks collapse, playing for distance.
- **In My Imagination** (2026): Endless Dreams: 3 minutes per image, auto-advance at 85%, 3 shared lives, monsters rising to ten.
- **Marble Land Grab** (2026): An endless io map with 40 rivals.

Variants:

- A hidden endless climb that opens after level 100 (Bix: The Rift)
- An unlockable or post-campaign Infinite Mode (Ink & Paper: DoodleCut; Delta Light 2)
- An endless marathon for up to 4 players (Critter Round-Up)

- Endless procedurally generated levels (RetroQix; Hundreds random levels)

- Clearing every hazard opens the whole photo and the run continues on a new photo (Photo Slice)

## Configurable rule parameters

These include clear threshold, lives, timer, enemy count and speed, starting energy, own-line deadliness, field size, special-ability charges, extra-life threshold, optional enemies or obstacles, and display or prize options. The rules themselves do not change, only their numbers or which optional pieces are present.

- **Zip & Zap** (1995): Additional Obstacles and player skill mode.
- **Penky** (1996): DIP switches set round time and the coverage threshold.
- **似非クイックス** (2003): Embed parameters: stage count, lives, goal %, Qix tail length, maximum Sparx count, Sparx spawn and speed-up intervals, game speed and colours.
- **Xonix (KolibriOS)** (2006): Field size setting.
- **PetsArtXoniX** (2019): Number of enemies, ball speed, lives and a sound toggle.
- **Traxx** (1983): Options set the game speed and the number of enemies on screen.

Variants:

## Roguelite runs with upgrade drafts

Play is organised as a run of consecutive stages, waves or a survivor-style session. Between stages, on level-up or at wave milestones the player drafts upgrades, often 1 of 3, that last for the rest of the run. Upgrades can be fed by XP from captured enemies, built into a deck of combinable abilities, or combined with curses and synergies.
