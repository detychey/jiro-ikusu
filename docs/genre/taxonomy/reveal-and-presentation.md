# Mechanics: reveal and presentation

The 43 mechanic families of the axis *reveal and presentation*: image reveal, silhouettes, themes, music, colour coding, visual feedback, juice, screen layout. The [taxonomy](../TAXONOMY.md) summarizes the axis and lists the 12 axes. **Games** counts the catalog games that show a family, as the merging agent counted them.

## Hidden-picture reveal

A picture lies under the unclaimed cover. Each claimed region shows its part of the picture instead of a flat fill, so capture progress reads as an image coming into view. Reaching the clear target usually shows most or all of the picture, and each level or stage brings a new one.

Games: 127 · lineages: qix, volfied, xonix, jezzball, gals-panic, amidar-painter, other

Examples:

- **Super Qix** (1987): Each of the 16 rounds hides a keyword picture under the field.
- **Volfied** (1989): Claimed area shows the next stage's picture.
- **Gals Panic** (1990): Claims reveal a hidden picture of a woman.
- **Youjuu Club** (1990): A demon marker uncovers a background image on Xonix rules; 80% revealed clears the stage.
- **Splash!** (1992): A brush erases a blue overlay on a painter grid to reveal a picture.
- **Lady Killer** (1993): Flipped tiles reveal the woman's picture.
- **Twin Qix** (1995): Each player's side reveals a different half of the level's picture.
- **Megami Tensei QIX Persona 3** (2007): Enclosed areas reveal official Persona 3 illustrations, set to Persona 3 BGM.
- **JezzBall Classic** (2011): Optional real photos (700) are placed into the built walls each level.
- **Sneak Peeling** (2020): Only an undetected cut peels away the black board to reveal the background.
- **Xonix Girls** (2026): A ship paints over territory to reveal the picture, a Gals Panic-style reveal on Xonix rules.
- **QixArt** (2026): Captured zones reveal fragments of a famous painting.

Variants:

- Photographs instead of drawn art: Maniax (cities around the world), Strong Lines (animals in 1991, city landscapes in 1994), Kwiks (true-colour photos), Fantasia and Gals Panic 3 (photographed models).
- Illustration and live-action picture modes in one game: Gal Pani II.
- The reveal is an option or a mode beside a plain fill: Mamba 2 (full-screen picture instead of the grey web pattern), Qix 2000 (Arrangement mode only); SeXoniX puts a picture where Xonix had fill patterns.
- Split or per-player pictures: Twin Qix (each player's side reveals a different half), Gals Panic 4 (each player uncovers a separate picture in versus).
- The revealed picture is the next stage's backdrop: Volfied.
- The reveal is a photo of the real territory uncovered: Pakal.
- Tile and block reveals: Lady Killer and Party Time: Gonta the Diver II (flipped tiles), Realm of Tile (tiles turn transparent), Crawlco Block Knockers (cleared blocks), SPLITRON (black tiles shatter), Qiks! (filled blocks), Wonder Stick (enclosed grid blocks vanish), 2nd Space (filled rectangles), Reef Reveal (square by square).
- Bombs, not enclosure, uncover the photo under the grid: The Return of Lady Frog.
- The player is a painting tool: PAINT'NT (a paintbrush paints the picture in), Splash! (a brush erases a blue overlay), Ink & Paper: DoodleCut (enclosed notebook areas get doodled), Xonix Girls (a ship paints over territory on Xonix rules).
- Photos are placed into the built walls rather than a cleared field: JezzBall Classic (700 optional real photos).
- The same picture every level as a running joke: JezzPaul (always a cat in a sink).
- Stealth reveal, where only an undetected cut peels the board away: Sneak Peeling.
- Licensed franchise art: Megami Tensei QIX Persona 3 (official illustrations set to Persona 3 BGM), Touhou QIX, Shakugan no Shana QIX, LewdQix (28 characters from different visual novels), Invincible Little Warrior (anime-character backgrounds).
- The art source is the theme: famous paintings (QixArt, Видатні художники світу), stained-glass windows (Gravebloom), DOS-era pixel art by Berlin underground designers (Metal Stuck), AI-generated animal photos, three per animal (CuteQix), retrowave backdrops (TERR1T0RY), hand-painted worlds (Cosmo Xonix, Starcutter), comic-style pictures (Comixxx Strip), glowing pictures (Glowfield), fantasy paintings (Hidden Fields).
- Per-character picture sets: Vivid Dolls (8 women, 3 images each), Cuties Hacked and Celebrities Hacked (12 sets each), In My Imagination (70 image stages), 땅따먹기 (GLORIOGO) (a themed photo shoot per stage).
- Non-adult subjects: Coach Tzabar (landscapes and vegetable still lifes), Pet Show and PetsArtXoniX (pets), Fly Trap (a storybook garden), Glimmer (paintings), Protean Girl (mostly clothed characters).
- The picture theme depends on the game mode: Xonix (RuStore).
- A random pick from a pool: Just4Qix (one of ten backgrounds), Photo Slice (a random device photo).
- Reveal packaged as an engine plugin: RPG Maker MZ Territory Action Plugin, Unveil.
- A counter of how much of the picture is revealed: Cartoon Xonix ('REVEALED %'), Territory Run ('SCAN' %).
- The clear threshold is stated as a share of the picture: Youjuu Club, PetsArtXoniX and Land Breaker (80%), In My Imagination (85%), HIDDEN JOURNEY: ASIA (80% of the mist).
- The reveal is framed by a silhouette guide or a themed cover (see those families): Gals Panic SS, Silhouette Stories, Gals Borderline, Panic (Y.O.S.), Pretty Girls Panic!, Bishoujo Battle Cyber Panic!.

## Progress HUD

An on-screen readout of capture progress: the claimed or revealed percentage, usually next to a timer, score, lives and level. Some HUDs show what remains instead, a target or bonus panel, side-objective meters, or sit outside or inside the playfield.

Games: 17 · lineages: qix, xonix, volfied, gals-panic, amidar-painter

Examples:

- **Xonix (Apple II, Matthias Gerhardt)** (1983): 'SCORE 00000 -- AREA 000 - STICKS 2' status line.
- **Antix** (1985): Score, percent cleared, lives and enemy count.
- **Illgen** (1989): A 'time pointer' bar beside a 'screen filled' bar.
- **Volfied (PC Engine port)** (1989): A side HUD outside the field.
- **Smiley** (1992): The HUD counts pictures ('Bild 1/…').
- **Cartoon Xonix** (2004): 'REVEALED: 51 %' counter.
- **Patchwork Heroes** (2010): Shows the percentage of the ship that remains.
- **Space Cropper** (2014): A '% left' counter.
- **Spherical Qix** (2022): The HUD sits on the back of the sphere, so you rotate it to read progress.
- **Gals Panic (Android)** (2024 or earlier): Live percentage vs 80%, TIME countdown and a skull-to-V-sign gauge.
- **LiM Grid Wars** (2025): Claimed percentage against a 'To conquer' target, plus an 'Active Bonus' panel.
- **Territory Run: Arcade Capture** (2026): A 'SCAN' percentage tracks how much of the hidden image is revealed.

Variants:

- A 'time pointer' bar beside a 'screen filled' bar: Illgen.
- A countdown next to fill %, score, lives and level: Strong Lines (1991); level, fill %, lives and points: Kwiks.
- A picture counter ('Bild 1/…'): Smiley.
- A status line with score, area and sticks: Xonix (Apple II).
- Enemy count beside percent cleared, score and lives: Antix.
- The share of the picture revealed: Cartoon Xonix ('REVEALED: 51 %'), Territory Run ('SCAN' %).
- What remains instead of what is claimed: Patchwork Heroes (percentage of the ship remaining), Space Cropper ('% left').
- Targets left: Spider Web (1989) ('Flys left').

## Restoration reveal (before/after)

The field shows a degraded version of the image (corrupted, blurred, desaturated, black-and-white or a 'before' picture), and claimed regions switch to the restored or 'after' version, so capture reads as repair rather than uncovering.

Games: 7 · lineages: qix

Examples:

- **Cacoma Knight in Bizyland** (1992): Each screen starts as a corrupted landscape; enclosing an area restores ('purifies') the original image there.
- **Vivid Dolls** (1998): Play starts inside a blurred image.
- **似非クイックス** (2003): Claimed areas switch from the stage's 'before' image to its 'after' image.
- **Chromonia** (2026): Each capture restores part of a desaturated public-domain painting.
- **World Tour Qix** (2026): Each level starts as a black-and-white postcard that captured territory shows in colour.
- **Zoooo: Classic Area Capture** (2026): Captures restore a grey illustration to colour.
- **VoidCutter** (2026): Each capture restores part of a landmark hidden under the Void.

Variants:

- Enclosing purifies a corrupted landscape back to the original: Cacoma Knight in Bizyland.
- Blurred to sharp: Vivid Dolls.
- A paired before and after image per stage: 似非クイックス.
- Desaturated or grey art restored to colour: Chromonia (public-domain paintings), Zoooo: Classic Area Capture (illustrations).
- A black-and-white postcard turns to colour: World Tour Qix.
- A landmark restored from under the Void: VoidCutter.
