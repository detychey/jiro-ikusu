# Mechanics: multiplayer and competition

The 41 mechanic families of the axis *multiplayer and competition*: versus, co-op, .io territory battles, asynchronous ghosts, leaderboards, battle royale, team play. The [taxonomy](../TAXONOMY.md) summarizes the axis and lists the 12 axes. **Games** counts the catalog games that show a family, as the merging agent counted them.

## Trail cut eliminates its owner

The standard io rule. While a player is outside their own territory, their open trail is vulnerable: any rival who touches it eliminates the trail's owner, and the player can kill rivals the same way by crossing their trails. Teammates may be exempt, and the cutter may be credited with the kill.

Games: 37 · lineages: io-territory

Examples:

- **Paper.io** (2016): An enemy touching your tail eliminates you.
- **Splix.io** (c. 2016 (unconfirmed)): Crashing into a rival's line while it is drawn kills them.
- **Frontiers.io** (2017): Anyone crossing your trail before enclosure kills you.
- **Paper.io 2** (2018): A rival hitting your exposed tail eliminates you.
- **Huaxian Quandi 2 (4399 rehost of Paper.io 2)** (2018): Cut their trails before they cut yours.
- **King.io: Conquer The World** (2021): Rivals attack your exposed tail.
- **Pyrodrome** (2025): Drive over an opponent's trail to cut it off.
- **Wrangler** (2025): Enemies touching your fragile rope kill you.
- **Grid Riders** (2026): Driving through a non-teammate's line destroys that player.
- **Marble Land Grab** (2026): A marble crossing your open path cuts you and you lose everything.
- **Pengu Floe** (2026): Cutting a rival's track drops it into the sea.
- **looprush** (2026): Having your trail cut kills you; the cutter gets the kill.

Variants:

- The cutter is credited with the kill (looprush).
- Only non-teammates' lines are lethal (Grid Riders).
- A cut costs the player everything they held (Marble Land Grab).
- The trail is a fragile rope (Wrangler) or a wet paint trail (Tint Turf).
- A cut rival drops into the sea (Pengu Floe).
- Vehicles drive over an opponent's trail to cut it off (Pyrodrome).
- A cut ends the round (Loop: Land Grab).
- Bots actively try to cut your trail (Ink Wars); rival crews intercept it (Kingpins.io).
- Robots are destroyed by stepping on their tail, and they destroy you the same way (Ttangttameokgi-wang).

## Simultaneous shared-field versus capture

Two or more players claim territory on one field at the same time, each in their own colour and with their own score. They contest the same free space and get in the way of each other's enclosures. A round ends on a timer, on full capture or at a coverage threshold, and the larger share or a composite score wins. The family includes local io-style matches on one machine, played on a shared keyboard, on gamepads or in split-screen.

Games: 31 · lineages: qix, xonix, jezzball, io-territory, amidar-painter, other

Examples:

- **Territoire** (1985): Two snakes compete for area.
- **Illgen** (1989): Two players claim at the same time on one field, each with their own score, lives and power bar.
- **Rampart** (1990): 2-player versus; the rival shoots your walls.
- **Jintori game (Matsuo & Takagi)** (1990): Players' programs compete on one field.
- **Harapeko Bakka** (1994): Up to 4 simultaneous players with a multitap; most eggs wins.
- **Penky** (1996): Versus over 1 bout or best of 3, won by coverage threshold or majority at time-out.
- **Live Wire!** (1999): Up to 4 players on one grid; score counts tiles held, tiles stolen and kills.
- **Battle Qix** (2002): Compete to occupy more territory than the opponent and win coins.
- **Paper.io 2** (2018): Steam version: local split-screen for up to 4.
- **Quarosel** (2023): Sabotage with your balls, counter a rival's cut mid-draw, get trapped as the field shrinks into a 'death pit'.
- **XONIX 1984 2 player remake** (2024): Both players claim land at the same time and can take land the other has claimed.
- **Coconut's Coco-Net** (2026): 1v1 arena, each player claiming area with JezzBall-style walls.

Variants:

- Holding more territory than the opponent wins coins (Battle Qix).

- Two players on one keyboard choose to cooperate or compete for space (KanwalAi Xonix clone).
- Up to 4 players in one arena on one screen (Fillit); up to 4 with a multitap, and the most eggs wins (Harapeko Bakka).

## Split-field race to quota

Each player clears their own copy or half of the field or picture at the same time, side by side, in split-screen or on linked machines. The first to reach the clear threshold wins the round, usually over a best-of-N match. Players affect each other only through the race, unless attacks are sent across (see 'Attacks sent to the opponent's field'). Versus rules are often eased, with unlimited lives or a lower quota.

Games: 8 · lineages: qix, gals-panic, volfied

Examples:

- **Qix (Game Boy)** (1990): Link-cable 2-player mode as Team Mario and Team Luigi, with a win or loss scene after each round.
- **Harem Challenge** (1995): Best of 3 or best of 5, with a lower area target for two players.
- **Twin Qix** (1995): Side-by-side fields; unlimited lives in versus; first to 95% wins, best of three.
- **Gals Panic 4** (1996): 2-player split-screen versus on separate pictures.
- **Gals Panic SS** (1996): Versus mode added in the home port.
- **Gals Panic S - Extra Edition** (1997): Left and right halves; first to claim 80% of the silhouette wins.
- **iSlash** (2010): Simultaneous slashing races on 50 multiplayer levels, added in a later version.
- **Landgrab - Line Cutting Arcade** (2026): Online races for 2-4 players on separate copies of one field; first to 80% wins.

Variants:

- Link-cable rounds played as Team Mario against Team Luigi, with a win or loss scene after each round (Qix, Game Boy).
- Two-player tournament over best of 3 or best of 5, with a lower area target in two-player play (Harem Challenge).

- Each player clears the left or right half of the same silhouette; the first to 80% wins (Gals Panic S - Extra Edition).
- Online races for 2-4 players on separate copies of one field; the first to 80% wins (Landgrab - Line Cutting Arcade).
- Real-time slashing races on 50 dedicated multiplayer levels, added in a later version (iSlash).

## Large-lobby arena

Many simultaneous players or bots share one map, from about 7 up to 90. The player is never alone with a single rival, and territory changes all over the board at once.

Games: 8 · lineages: io-territory
