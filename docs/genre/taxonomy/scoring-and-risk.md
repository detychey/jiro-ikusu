# Mechanics: scoring and risk

The 58 mechanic families of the axis *scoring and risk*: score systems and risk against reward: multipliers, slow-draw bonuses, percentage bonuses, perfect clears, time bonuses, combo chains, gambling on capture size. The [taxonomy](../TAXONOMY.md) summarizes the axis and lists the 12 axes. **Games** counts the catalog games that show a family, as the merging agent counted them.

## Overfill percentage bonus

Once the clear quota is met, usually because the final capture overshoots it, each extra percent claimed (or each percent less left to the enemy) pays bonus points. The bonus may be linear, exponential or capped. It rewards one more large, risky cut before the quota is reached.

Games: 28 · lineages: qix, xonix, volfied, jezzball

Examples:

- **Qix** (1981): A bonus for each 1% above the threshold.
- **Styx** (1983): 1,000 points per full percent above 80%.
- **Zolyx** (1987): Bonus points for every percent above 75%.
- **Volfied** (1989): The clear bonus rises toward 99.9%, up to 500,000.
- **Cacoma Knight in Bizyland** (1992): Clearing more than the Qualify percentage earns more points and rewards.
- **AQuix** (1997): Extra points and an extra life for finishing above 75%.
- **Land Breaker** (1999): Bonus for going above 80%.
- **IceBreaker** (2000): An exponentially growing bonus beyond 80%.
- **Xonix Blast** (2012): Overfills earn extra points spent in the shop.
- **Jezzball v1.0 (TI-83 Plus / TI-84 Plus, Kerm Martian)** (2013): Bonus proportional to the fill past 65%.
- **Trappy Pixel** (2023): The bonus grows with fill above 75%.
- **Eight Bit Qix** (c. 2026): 500 points per percent over the goal.

## Slow-draw score multiplier

The player can draw at a slow speed as well as a fast one. Area closed using only the slow line scores a fixed multiple (2x to 10x) of fast-drawn area, which trades longer exposure of the open line for points. Slow-drawn areas usually get their own fill colour.
