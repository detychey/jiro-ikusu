# Concept ideas

142 ideas for growing the gameplay variety of Jiro-ikusu, drawn from the mechanics of the 710 games of [`CATALOG.md`](CATALOG.md). They are proposals, not plans: the project [README](../../README.md) is the specification, and [`README.md`](README.md) explains how the ideas were made and gives the recommended shortlist.

Three judges scored each idea from 1 to 5: a core engineer (feasibility and cost), a fairness judge (D14 and the gap between search bots and humans in paid play) and a game designer (play value). **Score** is their sum, out of 15; a one-point difference is noise. **Gap** is the fairness judge's view of the gap between a search bot and a skilled human: it narrows, stays neutral, widens, or is unclear. The tier is the core engineer's; an idea's page says when it differs from the proposer's. Each idea's page gives its rule sketch, inspirations, budget and the three judgments in full.

Tiers, by what an idea touches:

- **T0 Presentation:** client only: rendering, palettes, sound, effects, UI. No consensus impact.
- **T1 Level data v1:** new levels or level sets on the existing knobs: new level ids, a playtest and a calibration, no rule change.
- **T2 Level format v2:** new per-level fields that parameterize existing rules: a format version and new vectors, usually with T3.
- **T3 New core rule:** a new entity, cell state, item or capture rule: a RULES_VERSION bump, README §4 text, new vectors, a new state hash version for a new state, bots that learn it, recalibration.
- **T4 Input or journal:** uses Y or mask bit 7: journal v2, and revisits D8.
- **T5 Mode, scoring or contracts:** new modes, Outcome, IReferee or Season changes, new economics, Superchallenge.

| 12 | [Topology season: one field shape per slot](ideas/v1-levels.md#topology-season-one-field-shape-per-slot) | neutral | A season whose 16 levels are 16 different kinds of field, instead of mostly the open field with harder knobs, so each 6-level Challenge draw shows about five different kinds of board. |

## T2 Level format v2 (5)

| 12 | [Mixed-speed minion roster](ideas/field-enemies.md#mixed-speed-minion-roster) | neutral | Minions on one level no longer share a pace. A slow sentinel and a fast runner patrol the same rail, so the gaps between them open and close on a rhythm the player can learn to read. |

| 12 | [Figure ground: only marked cells count](ideas/capture-rules.md#figure-ground-only-marked-cells-count) | unclear | Only the shape outlined on the field counts toward the target. The rest is just ground to cut through, and the best play shuts the boss outside the shape. |
| 12 | [Last-life lifeline on the rail](ideas/items-and-power-ups.md#last-life-lifeline-on-the-rail) | narrows | When a hit leaves you on your last life with at least half the target claimed, a lifeline appears on a far rail cell. | 12 | [Sweepers on fixed tracks](ideas/terrain-and-geometry.md#sweepers-on-fixed-tracks) | neutral | Small hazards run back and forth along straight tracks drawn on the field and cut any trail they touch. | 11 | [Barbed trail after a big capture](ideas/items-and-power-ups.md#barbed-trail-after-a-big-capture) | narrows | A single capture of at least N cells arms your next trail with a barb. | 11 | [Beat-pattern boss](ideas/controls-time-and-pacing.md#beat-pattern-boss) | narrows | Instead of a steady pace, the boss moves in a rhythm: a burst of moves, then rests, repeating every bar. The rests are windows for a quick cut, and the beat is shown on the boss and heard in the browser. |
| 11 | [Counter-patrolling warden](ideas/items-and-power-ups.md#counter-patrolling-warden) | narrows | A friendly warden walks the rail the opposite way round from the minions and destroys any minion it meets. | 11 | [Escalation at claim thresholds](ideas/level-structure-and-progression.md#escalation-at-claim-thresholds) | widens | Some minions sleep until your claim passes a marked line, then announce their return with a flashing spawn. ## Rejected (2)

An idea is rejected when the core engineer finds it infeasible or the fairness judge finds it unacceptable.

- [Bank it: a level that never clears](ideas/v1-levels.md#bank-it-a-level-that-never-clears) (`v1-levels#11`, T1 Level data v1, score 8): the fairness judge found it unacceptable in Challenge. The reference mix's median claim falls under the claim floor of [`levels/targets.json`](../../levels/targets.json), so its weight clamps at about 15 times an ordinary level's, and drawing it would decide rankings. It also fails `playtest --check` (P11). It stays possible as a Training-only board.
- [Twin bosses and the split multiplier](ideas/scoring-and-risk.md#twin-bosses-and-the-split-multiplier) (`scoring-and-risk#14`, T5 Mode, scoring or contracts, score 7): the fairness judge found it unacceptable. The 2× multiplier after a split pays for timing a cut between two seeded bosses, where perfect foresight pays most: search would split on every two-boss level, while calibration sees only the mix, which rarely splits. The core engineer also found D7's coverage property false after a split.
